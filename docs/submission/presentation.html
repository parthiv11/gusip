Gujarat Police Innovation Challenge 2026

One wall. The cameras stay where they are.

GUSIP is a statewide picture of CCTV — not a new NVR. Ahmedabad Traffic, Surat Municipal, highway, railway, and the official Sentinel feeds keep recording locally. We only lift plates, locations, and short evidence when something matches a watchlist.

Tonight: a white Fortuner GJ 01 ST 0001 is stolen in Paldi. Six minutes later it is on SG Highway, then the Gandhinagar link. One operator sees the hops. Nobody phones three control rooms.

Running now: 50 departmental-style cameras + 31 official Sentinel feeds (Chimanbhai Bridge, Suvidha Park, Dolatpara…). Path to 80,000 is more adapters and regional GPUs — not 80,000 extra hard disks at SCRB.

What is broken today

The estate

Cameras already exist. They sit on different VMS boxes, different vendors, different passwords. An IO in Ahmedabad cannot see a Surat hit unless someone WhatsApps a still.

The temptation

Buy one giant statewide VMS, copy every stream to Gandhinagar, and run AI in the middle. That is Model 4. It is the most expensive way to lose departmental ownership and fill a WAN.

The actual job

Find a plate. Follow it across junctions. Keep an audit of who searched and why. Do it in seconds, not after the FIR is typed.

Our rule

Video stays at the NVR that already paid for the disk. GUSIP takes the event: time, camera, plate, one snapshot, map pin.

How an operator actually uses it

  1. Sign in as operator. The wall defaults to Gov feeds — Chimanbhai Bridge is already playing from live.sentinelgujarat.in.
  2. A watchlist hit lands. The camera jumps into the large pane. Repeat hits on the same camera become ×27, not 27 cards.
  3. Yellow numbered pins on the map are open alerts. Click one: screenshot evidence, plate, confidence.
  4. Investigate GJ 01 ST 0001, purpose = stolen vehicle. GIS draws Paldi → SG Highway → Gandhinagar.
  5. A coordinator in Ahmedabad only sees home-district cameras until they type an FIR reason and take a 30-minute break-glass.

Operators cannot download CSV. Investigators can. Every search writes purpose, user, and IP to the audit trail.

If you only remember four numbers

< 8 seconds

Stolen-corridor demo: five cameras, Ahmedabad west to Gandhinagar, plate in the inbox before the eighth second.

31 official feeds

Jury-provided Sentinel cameras, live in the Gov-feeds tab. Not a stock video. Sync Sentinel, click SEN-1.

4 roles, 1 purpose

Operator, IO, coordinator, admin. Search will not run until you pick why (stolen, wanted, evaluation…).

0 extra NVRs

At 80,000 cameras we still do not store 80,000 bitstreams. We store events. Disk stays with the department that already bought it.
